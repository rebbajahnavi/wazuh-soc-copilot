DOCUMENT: Incident Response Lifecycle Overview
SOURCE: NIST SP 800-61 Rev 2
SECTION: Preparation and Analysis
CONTENT:
The incident response lifecycle comprises four main stages:
1. Preparation: Establishing incident response capabilities, hardening systems, securing endpoints, and continuous training.
2. Detection and Analysis: Monitoring events, validating alerts through SIEM correlation, assessing incident scope, and determining severity.
3. Containment, Eradication, and Recovery:
   - Containment: Implementing short-term isolation (e.g., isolating endpoint network interface, blocking malicious IP addresses) followed by long-term containment strategies.
   - Eradication: Identifying all affected hosts, deleting artifacts, disabling compromised accounts, and closing vulnerability gaps.
   - Recovery: Restoring systems from trusted backups, validating clean operation, and returning systems to production with enhanced telemetry.
4. Post-Incident Activity: Documenting root cause, detailing attacker behavior, refining rules, and updating defensive playbooks.
